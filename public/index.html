Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need an account to post?

No. Anyone can post a task without creating an account. You just need to provide contact info and a government ID for verification.

How does payment work?

Choose cash or check when posting. Once the student finishes and you mark it done, you pay them directly. No card, no fees for posters.

Who are the students?

All students are 16–24 years old, verified with a school or k12 email, and agree to community guidelines before joining.

What if the work isn't done right?

You control when you pay. If unsatisfied, hold off on payment and email us at support@campushands.app — we'll help mediate.

Is my personal info safe?

Yes. Your home address is never shown publicly. Full addresses are only revealed to the student you specifically accept.

What jobs are not allowed?

Electrical, plumbing, roofing, chainsaw use, weapons, hazardous chemicals, medical care, or anything illegal. See our Community Guidelines for the full list.

How old do babysitters need to be?

Workers must be 18+ to apply for babysitting and childcare jobs. Campus Hands enforces this automatically at the application stage.

How much does Campus Hands charge?

Nothing. Campus Hands is completely free — no fees for posters and no fees for students. You agree on the pay when the task is posted, and the poster pays the student that full amount in cash or by check when the work is done.
